29. (Peano 定理) 设  \( f(x) \)  在  \( x_{0} \)  有  \( n(n \geq 1) \)  阶导数，在含  \( x_{0} \)  的区间内有一点  \( x_{0} \)  到  \( n-1 \)  阶导数，则  \( f(x) = T_{n}(x) + o\left((x - x_{0})^{n}\right) \)  ( \( x \rightarrow x_{0} \) ).

其中  \( T_{n}(x) = a_{0} + a_{1}(x - x_{0}) + \cdots + a_{n}(x - x_{0})^{n} \) ,  \( T_{n}^{(k)}(x_{0}) = f^{(k)}(x_{0}) \) .

证明：

 \( \lim_{x \to x_{0}} \frac{f(x) - T_{n}(x)}{(x - x_{0})^{n}} = \lim_{x \to x_{0}} \frac{f^{(n-1)}(x) - T_{n}^{(n-1)}(x)}{n!(x - x_{0})^{n}} \)

 \( \lim_{x \to x_{0}} \frac{f^{(n-1)}(x) - \left[f^{(n-1)}(x_{0}) + f^{(n)}(x_{0})(x - x_{0})\right]}{n!(x - x_{0})^{n}} \)

 \( = \frac{1}{n!} \left[ \lim_{x \to x_{0}} \frac{f^{(n-1)}(x) - f^{(n)}(x_{0})}{x - x_{0}} - f^{(n)}(x_{0}) \right] = 0 \)

(错误解法： \( = \lim_{x \to x_{0}} \frac{f^{(n)}(x) - T_{n}^{(n)}(x)}{n!} = \frac{f^{(n)}(x_{0}) - T_{n}^{(n)}(x_{0})}{n!} = 0 \) )
