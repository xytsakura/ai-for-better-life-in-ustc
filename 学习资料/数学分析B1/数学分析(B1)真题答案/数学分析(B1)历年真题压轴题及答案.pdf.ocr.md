We prove a more general statement: If  \( \alpha \in (0,1] \)  and we have for all  \( x, y \in R \)  that  \( |f'(x) - f'(y)| \leq |x - y|^{\alpha} \) , then it holds for all  \( x \in R \)  that  \( |f'(x)|^{\frac{1+\alpha}{\alpha}} < \frac{1 + \alpha}{\alpha} f(x) \) .


Note first that for all  \( x, y \in R \)  we have that


 \[ f(y)-f(x)=\int_{0}^{1}(y-x)f^{\prime}(ty+(1-t)x)dt=f^{\prime}(x)(y-x)+\int_{0}^{1}(f^{\prime}(ty+(1-t)x)-f^{\prime}(x))(y-x)dt. \]


Now, the assumption implies that


 \[ \left|\int_{0}^{1}\left(f^{\prime}(ty+(1-t)x)-f^{\prime}(x)\right)(y-x)dt\right|\leq|x-y|\int_{0}^{1}t^{\alpha}|x-y|^{\alpha}dt=\frac{1}{\alpha+1}|x-y|^{1+\alpha}. \]


Hence, we obtain for all x, y that


 \[ f(y)\leq f(x)+(y-x)f^{\prime}(x)+\frac{1}{1+\alpha}|x-y|^{1+\alpha}. \]


Setting here  \(  y = x - |f'(x)|^{\frac{1}{\alpha}-1} f'(x)  \)  yields


 \[ 0<f(y)\leq f(x)-|f^{\prime}(x)|^{\frac{1}{\alpha}+1}+\frac{1}{1+\alpha}|f^{\prime}(x)|^{\frac{1+\alpha}{\alpha}}=f(x)-\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha}|f^{\prime}(x)|^{\frac{1+\alpha}{\alpha}}. \]


This completes the proof.
